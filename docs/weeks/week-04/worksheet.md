## 7. If the Core Component Works

Improve what you have before adding anything new:

- give the component one more useful prop
- extract a second component, such as a header or footer
- make one component remember something about itself, as in Part 7
- improve how your component looks using CSS you already wrote
- use the supplied poster artwork in your cards

You are not expected to rebuild your whole Week 2 interface in React today, or
to restore your Week 3 interaction. That is Week 5's work.
